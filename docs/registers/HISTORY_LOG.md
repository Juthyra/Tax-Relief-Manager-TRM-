## How to read this log

- **Type:** Fact (verified from a source), Decision (made by the owner), Issue (a problem or defect), Risk, Open (unresolved), Process (a step performed).
- **Status:** Open, Resolved, Verified, Unverified, Flagged (I made a call that needs the owner's yes or no).
- **Rules:** every new finding gets the next ID and is appended; nothing is deleted, only its status changes. Every working session adds its prompt, its process steps and its findings here, and updates the change log if a URS or SRS document changed.
